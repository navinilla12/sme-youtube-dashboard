A high-fidelity, interactive Streamlit dashboard built for executive reporting. Features dynamic cross-filtering, multi-tier categorization, and granular viewer retention depth tracking.

## 📊 Overview
The **SME Media YouTube Analytics Engine** is an enterprise-grade analytics prototype developed to track `@SMEMedia` channel performance, audience engagement rates, and viewer retention depth. Designed for executive stakeholder review.

### Key Features
* **Interactive Cross-Filtering:** Click on any video bar or category pie slice to instantly isolate performance metrics across the board, or click empty space to reset.
* **Viewer Retention Depth:** Granular tracking for minimum, average, and maximum viewer watch durations (in seconds) alongside catalog views and engagement metrics.
* **Audience Likes & Engagement:** Dedicated visual modules mapping audience likes alongside retention depth and engagement percentages.
* **Granular Content Records:** Full content breakdown showing publication schedules, total video durations, and performance tiers.
